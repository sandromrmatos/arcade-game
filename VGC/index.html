Game Rules

Team Building

  • Build a team of 6 unique creatures
  • For each creature:
    • Allocate 30 stat points (max 20 per stat)
    • Choose 4 moves from 12 available
    • Select 1 ability from 2 options

Pre-Battle Selection

  • Both players see each other's full teams
  • Select 4 creatures to bring into battle
  • First 2 selected start as active
  • Remaining 2 start on the bench

Battle Structure

  • Each player has 2 active creatures and 2 bench creatures
  • Each turn, choose an action for each active creature:
    • Fight: Use one of the creature's 4 moves
    • Switch: Swap with a bench creature
  • Switching happens before moves
  • Switched-in creatures cannot act that turn
  • Battle ends when all 4 creatures of one side faint
  • Maximum 30 turns

Move Priority & Speed

  • Moves execute in order of:
    1. Priority (higher priority goes first)
    2. Speed (higher speed goes first)
    3. Random tiebreaker
  • Most moves have priority 1
  • Some special moves have priority 2 or 3

Damage Calculation

  • Physical moves: Damage = Power × (Attack / Defense)
  • Special moves: Damage = Power × (Special Attack / Special Defense)
  • STAB: +20% damage when move type matches creature type
  • Super Effective: +25% damage
    • Mystic → Wind
    • Wind → Celestial
    • Celestial → Mystic
    • Mechanic → Neutral

Stat Changes

  • Some moves increase or decrease stats
  • Stat changes persist until creature faints or switches out
  • Maximum +80% increase (1.8x)
  • Maximum -80% decrease (0.2x)

Targeting Types

  • Single: Hit one opponent creature
  • Double: Hit both opponent creatures
  • Self: Affect only the user
  • Self and Partner: Affect both allied creatures
