GAME_SPEC_VERSION: 1.0
GAME_TITLE: "2v2 Creature Battle System"

========================================
1. TEAM BUILDING RULES
========================================

[TEAM_COMPOSITION]
- Each player builds a team of 6 unique creatures.
- For each creature:
  - Choose 4 moves out of the 12 it can learn.
  - Choose 1 ability out of the 2 it can have.
  - Distribute 30 stat points across the 6 stats:
    - Stats: HP, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, Speed.
    - No more than 20 points may be assigned to any single stat.
    - Points are integers.
    - Points add directly to the creature's base stats.

[PRE_BATTLE_SELECTION]
- Both players see each other's full team of 6 creatures.
- Each player selects 4 creatures to bring to battle.
- The first 2 selected start as active creatures.
- The remaining 2 start on the bench.
- Player has 1 minute to choose; AI picks 4 randomly if time expires.

========================================
2. BATTLE STRUCTURE
========================================

[ACTIVE_AND_BENCH]
- Each player has:
  - 2 active creatures (on the field).
  - 2 bench creatures (available to switch in).
- Bench creatures can be switched in during a turn.
- A switched-in creature cannot act on the turn it enters.

[SWITCHING_RULES]
- Switching counts as the creature's action for the turn.
- Both active creatures may switch on the same turn.
- Switching happens before any moves resolve.
- Damage for that turn is applied to the newly switched-in creatures.

[TURN_FLOW]
1. Player chooses actions for both active creatures:
   - Each active creature chooses either:
     - A move (from its 4 selected moves), or
     - A switch (to a bench creature).
2. AI chooses actions for its active creatures (same options).
3. All four creatures act in the same turn.
4. Move order is determined by:
   - Move priority (higher priority acts first).
   - Speed (higher Speed acts first among same priority).
   - Random tie-breaker if speeds match.
5. After all moves resolve, the next turn begins.
6. Turn counter increments; maximum 30 turns (game can end in draw or custom rule if needed).

[KNOCKOUTS]
- A creature reaching 0 HP faints (is KO'd).
- If a creature is KO'd before its move, its move is cancelled.
- When all 4 creatures of a player faint, that player loses.
- If both players lose simultaneously, the result is a draw.

========================================
3. MOVE MECHANICS
========================================

[MOVE_DATA]
Each move has:
- Name
- Type: one of {Mystic, Wind, Celestial, Mechanic, Neutral}
- Power (numeric)
- Accuracy (percentage, e.g., 75 means 75% chance to hit)
- Category:
  - Physical (uses Attack vs Defense)
  - Special (uses Special Attack vs Special Defense)
- Priority: integer in [0, 3]
- Targeting type (see TARGETING_TYPES)
- Secondary effects (see STAT_CHANGES & FIELD_EFFECTS sections)

[ACCURACY]
- A move with X% accuracy has (100 - X)% chance to fail.
- If a move fails:
  - No damage is dealt.
  - No secondary effects occur.

[TARGETING_TYPES]
Supported targeting types:
- Single target:
  - Player chooses one opponent active slot (left or right).
- Double target:
  - Hits both opponent active creatures (left and right).
- Self:
  - Affects only the user.
- Self and partner:
  - Affects the user and its allied active partner.
- Other three:
  - Affects partner + both opponents (3 total, unless slots are empty).

[EMPTY_SLOT_REDIRECTION]
- If a single-target move is aimed at an empty opponent slot:
  - It automatically redirects to the opponent's other active creature (if present).

[DOUBLE_TARGET_RESOLUTION]
- For double-target moves:
  - Hit left slot first, then right slot.
  - If the left slot creature is KO'd by the first hit, the second hit still applies to the right slot.
  - If only one opponent creature is alive, only that creature is hit.

========================================
4. DAMAGE CALCULATION
========================================

[BASE_DAMAGE_FORMULA]

Let:
- P = Move Power
- A = Attacker's relevant offensive stat
- D = Defender's relevant defensive stat

For Physical moves:
- Offensive stat: Attack
- Defensive stat: Defense
- Formula:
  - Damage_raw = P * (A / D)

For Special moves:
- Offensive stat: Special Attack
- Defensive stat: Special Defense
- Formula:
  - Damage_raw = P * (A / D)

[ROUNDING_AND_HP]
- Damage_raw is rounded to an integer (e.g., standard rounding or floor; choose and be consistent).
- Defender's HP is reduced by Damage (post-modifiers).
- HP cannot go below 0.

[SAME_TYPE_ATTACK_BONUS (STAB)]
- If the type of the move matches the type of the creature using it:
  - The move's Power is increased by 20% before applying the damage formula.
  - New_Power = floor_or_round(Power * 1.2) (implementation choice, but consistent).
- STAB is applied before type effectiveness and before rounding final damage.

[TYPE_EFFECTIVENESS]
- Super effective hits deal +25% damage (after base damage and STAB, then rounded).
- Super Effective Chart:
  - Mystic -> Wind (Mystic-type moves are super effective against Wind-type creatures)
  - Wind -> Celestial
  - Celestial -> Mystic
  - Mechanic -> Neutral
  - Neutral -> none (no super effective targets)
- Effectiveness is based only on the move's type, not the creature's type.
- Implementation:
  - If move is super effective vs defender type:
    - Damage_effective = round(Damage_base * 1.25)
  - Otherwise:
    - Damage_effective = Damage_base

========================================
5. STAT CHANGES & SECONDARY EFFECTS
========================================

[SECONDARY_EFFECTS]
Secondary effects of moves can:
- Increase own stats.
- Decrease own stats.
- Increase opponent's stats.
- Decrease opponent's stats.
- Cause self-damage (e.g., recoil).

[STAT_CHANGE_RULES]
- Stat changes apply as percentage modifiers to the current stats (implementation detail: store as multipliers).
- Stat changes reset when switching out.
- Stat changes persist until:
  - The creature switches out, or
  - The creature faints.
- Stat changes may stack but are capped:
  - Maximum +80% total increase (i.e., up to 1.8x of base stat).
  - Maximum -80% total decrease (i.e., down to 0.2x of base stat).
- Example:
  - If a creature gets +50% Attack and then +40% Attack:
    - Total = +90%, but cap at +80%.
  - If a creature gets -50% Defense and then -40% Defense:
    - Total = -90%, but cap at -80%.

========================================
6. FIELD EFFECTS
========================================

[FIELD_EFFECT_CREATION]
- Certain moves or abilities may create field effects.

[FIELD_EFFECT_BEHAVIOR]
- Field effects apply to all active creatures on the affected player's side.
- They persist for a fixed number of turns (duration specified by effect).
- They are not tied to a specific creature:
  - They remain even if the creator faints or switches out.
- Any creature switched in is immediately affected by active field effects.

[FIELD_EFFECT_EXAMPLES]
- Example 1:
  - "Player's creatures' Speed increased by 50% for 5 turns."
- Example 2:
  - "Computer's creatures' Attack increased by 5% for 3 turns."

========================================
7. ABILITIES
========================================

[ABILITY_TYPES]
Abilities fall into two categories:

1. Switch-in abilities:
   - Trigger when the creature enters an active slot.
   - Includes:
     - When the battle starts (initial active creatures).
     - When switching in from the bench.

2. Passive abilities:
   - Always active while the creature is in an active slot.
   - Examples:
     - Immunity to certain move types.
     - Stat modifiers (e.g., +10% Speed).
     - Accuracy modifiers (e.g., +5% accuracy to all moves).

[ABILITY_EFFECTS]
- Abilities may modify:
  - Stats (flat or percentage).
  - Accuracy (global or specific move types).
  - Damage (e.g., boost certain types).
  - Field effects (create, extend, or alter).
  - Other battle mechanics (e.g., priority changes, immunity, etc.).

========================================
8. REQUIRED DATA FILES
========================================

[CREATURES_CSV]
File: Creatures.csv
Each row represents one creature and contains:
- Name (string)
- Type (one of {Mystic, Wind, Celestial, Mechanic, Neutral})
- PNG asset path or identifier (string)
- Base stats:
  - HP
  - Attack
  - Defense
  - Special Attack
  - Special Defense
  - Speed
- List of 12 learnable moves (move names, comma-separated or structured list)
- List of 2 abilities (ability names)

[MOVES_CSV]
File: Moves.csv
Each row represents one move and contains:
- Name (string)
- Type (Mystic, Wind, Celestial, Mechanic, Neutral)
- Power (integer)
- Accuracy (integer percentage)
- Secondary effects (text description or structured effect data)
- Targeting type (Single, Double, Self, SelfAndPartner, OtherThree)
- Category (Physical / Special)
- Priority (integer 0–3)

[ABILITIES_CSV]
File: Abilities.csv
Each row represents one ability and contains:
- Name (string)
- Description of effect (text or structured rules)

========================================
9. VISUAL / UI REQUIREMENTS
========================================

[UI_ELEMENTS]
The UI must show at all times:
- Player's 2 active creatures:
  - Name
  - Type
  - Current HP / Total HP
  - Status indicators (optional, if implemented).
- Player's 2 bench creatures:
  - Name
  - Type
  - Current HP / Total HP (if visible).
- Opponent's 2 active creatures:
  - Name
  - Type
  - Current HP / Total HP (or at least visible HP bar).
- Turn counter:
  - Display current turn number.
  - Maximum 30 turns.
- Field effects:
  - Show active field effects on each side.
  - Show remaining duration (turns left).
- Info button:
  - When clicked, shows a summary of all rules in this spec:
    - Team building rules.
    - Battle structure.
    - Move mechanics.
    - Damage calculation.
    - Stat changes.
    - Field effects.
    - Abilities.
    - Data file structure.

[PLAYER_INPUT]
- Player can:
  - Select moves for each active creature.
  - Select switch actions to bench creatures.
  - Confirm actions for the turn.
- AI:
  - Chooses actions for its active creatures (can be random or strategic).

========================================
10. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (FOR LLM)
========================================

[CORE OBJECTS]
- Creature:
  - name, type, base_stats, allocated_stats, current_stats, current_hp, moves, ability, stat_modifiers, status.
- Move:
  - name, type, power, accuracy, category, priority, targeting_type, secondary_effects.
- Ability:
  - name, description, type (switch-in or passive), effect logic.
- FieldEffect:
  - owner_side (player or AI), effect_type, magnitude, duration_remaining, affected_stats_or_mechanics.

[TURN_RESOLUTION ORDER]
1. Apply any start-of-turn effects (if implemented).
2. Determine actions (player + AI).
3. Resolve switches:
   - Apply switch-in abilities for newly active creatures.
4. Determine move order:
   - Sort actions by:
     - Priority (descending).
     - Speed (descending).
     - Random tie-breaker.
5. For each action in order:
   - Check if user is still alive (HP > 0); if not, skip.
   - Check accuracy for moves.
   - Apply damage and secondary effects.
   - Check for KO and handle fainting.
6. End-of-turn:
   - Decrement field effect durations.
   - Remove expired field effects.
   - Increment turn counter.
   - Check win/loss/draw conditions.

[WIN_CONDITION]
- If all 4 creatures of one side are KO'd:
  - Opponent wins.
- If both sides lose all 4 creatures on the same resolution step:
  - Result is a draw.
- Optional: if turn counter reaches 30:
  - Decide outcome (e.g., compare remaining HP, or declare draw).
