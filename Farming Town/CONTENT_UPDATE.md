## Recipe Dependencies & Production Chains

### Chain 1: Wheat → Corn → Animal Feed → Chicken/Eggs
```
Wheat (Level 1) → Plant
Corn (Level 5) → Plant
↓
Wheat + Corn (Level 6) → Animal Feed (Mill)
↓
Animal Feed (Level 6) → Chicken & Eggs (Chicken Farm)
```

### Chain 2: Cinema Products
```
Corn (Level 5) → Popcorn (Cinema, Level 11)
Onion (Level 11) → Onion Rings (Cinema, Level 12)
Orange (Level 8 reward) → Orange Juice (Cinema, Level 13)
```

### Chain 3: Restaurant Stew
```
Tomato (Level 3) → Plant
Carrot (Level 10) → Plant
Potato (Level 6) → Plant
Onion (Level 11) → Plant
Cow (Level 9) → Steak (Butcher, Level 10)
↓
All ingredients → Stew (Restaurant, Level 11)
```

## Economic Balance

### Investment Analysis

#### Corn (Level 5)
- **Investment**: 8 coins
- **Return**: 36 coins (3 × 12) + 4 XP + 15 XP (3 × 5)
- **Profit**: 28 coins, 19 XP
- **ROI**: 350%
- **Time**: 4 hours
- **Coins/Hour**: 7

#### Carrot (Level 10)
- **Investment**: 35 coins
- **Return**: 64 coins (2 × 32) + 15 XP + 36 XP (2 × 18)
- **Profit**: 29 coins, 51 XP
- **ROI**: 83%
- **Time**: 18 hours
- **Coins/Hour**: 1.6

#### Onion (Level 11)
- **Investment**: 50 coins
- **Return**: 108 coins (4 × 27) + 17 XP + 80 XP (4 × 20)
- **Profit**: 58 coins, 97 XP
- **ROI**: 116%
- **Time**: 36 hours
- **Coins/Hour**: 1.6

#### Animal Feed Production
- **Investment**: 1 wheat (1 coin) + 2 corn (16 coins) = 17 coins
- **Return**: 60 coins (3 × 20) + 10 XP + 18 XP (3 × 6)
- **Profit**: 43 coins, 28 XP
- **ROI**: 253%
- **Time**: 3 hours
- **Coins/Hour**: 14.3

#### Stew Production
- **Investment**: 1 tomato + 2 carrots + 1 potato + 1 steak + 2 onions
- **Cost**: ~4 + 70 + 10 + (varies) + 100 = ~184+ coins
- **Return**: 240 coins (2 × 120) + 35 XP + 90 XP (2 × 45)
- **Profit**: ~56 coins, 125 XP
- **Time**: 6 hours
- **Complexity**: Very high (requires 5+ other productions first)
