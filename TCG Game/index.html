Elemental Awakening - How to Play

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to collect 3 Prize Points by knocking out your opponent's creatures!

🎴 Deck Building

Custom Decks: You can create your own custom decks! Click "Create Custom Deck" from the deck selection screen.

  • Each deck must have exactly 20 cards
  • You can mix one primary type (Mystic, Celestial, Wind, or Mechanic) with Neutral cards
  • Item cards can be mixed with any deck type
  • Evolved creatures require their base form in the deck

⚡ Energy System

  • Attach 1 energy per turn to any creature in play
  • Energy is required to use attacks (check the move cost)
  • Energy stays on creatures even when they retreat to the bench

🎮 Turn Actions

On your turn, you can perform these actions (in any order):

  • Play creatures from your hand to empty bench slots
  • Evolve creatures (after turn 3) by playing evolution cards on matching creatures
  • Attach 1 energy to a creature in play
  • Use 1 item card (Potion, Booster, Card Draw, Power-Up)
  • Use abilities from creatures in play (once per turn per creature)
  • Retreat your active creature to the bench (costs energy)
  • Attack once with your active creature (ends your turn)

Note: Abilities must be used before attacking, as attacking ends your turn.

✨ Abilities

Some creatures have special abilities that can be activated during your turn:

  • Ability Buttons: Creatures with abilities show a button below the card
  • Once Per Turn: Each creature can use its ability once per turn
  • Location Requirements: Some abilities require the creature to be in the active spot, while others work from anywhere (active or bench)
  • Before Attacking: Abilities must be used before you attack, since attacking ends your turn
  • Greyed Out: Ability buttons are disabled when conditions aren't met or already used this turn

Example Abilities:

  • Defense Aura (Antheara) - Active only: Reduce opponent's next attack damage by 10
  • Resourceful Recovery (Serafina) - Any location: Flip a coin, if heads attach energy to a random creature you control

⚔️ Combat

  • Attacking ends your turn automatically
  • When a creature's damage equals or exceeds its HP, it's knocked out
  • Each knockout earns you 1 Prize Point

🔥 Type Effectiveness

Super Effective Matchups: When a move from one type hits a creature of a super-effective type, it deals an additional +10 damage.

  • Mystic is super effective against Wind 🔮→💨
  • Wind is super effective against Celestial 💨→🌿
  • Celestial is super effective against Mystic 🌿→🔮
  • Mechanic is super effective against Neutral ⚙️→⚪
  • Neutral has no super effective matchups

Note: The +10 damage bonus stacks with other damage bonuses like Booster, Power-Up, and move effects!

🌟 Special Conditions

Hallucination 😵:

  • Caused by certain Mystic-type moves (Mind Mirage, Ethereal Echo, Phantom Pulse, Dreamwave, Dream Booster)
  • When attacking while hallucinating, flip a coin:
  • ✓ Heads: Deal 10 extra damage
  • ✗ Tails: Deal normal damage + 40 damage to yourself
  • Cured when: The creature moves from active slot to bench (retreat, swap, or forced swap)

🎁 Item Cards

  • Potion: Heal a creature for 20 HP
  • Card Draw: Draw 2 cards from your deck
  • Booster: Your next attack deals +20 damage
  • Power-Up: Your next 3 attacks (over 3 turns) deal +10 damage each

🏆 Winning the Game

  • Collect 3 Prize Points by knocking out opponent's creatures
  • Each creature knockout = 1 point (including bench creatures)
  • If your opponent has no creatures left to play, you win
  • After 30 turns, the player with more points wins

💡 Tips

  • Build up energy on bench creatures while your active creature battles
  • Retreat low-HP creatures to safety before they're knocked out
  • Use item cards strategically to gain advantage
  • Watch out for type weaknesses and resistances
  • Some moves have special effects - read them carefully!
  • Bench creatures can be knocked out by AoE moves like Mystic Blaze
